About the App

Problem Tracker is an internal tool for reporting, tracking, and resolving technical issues. It supports multiple roles including customers, solvers, and administrators.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • I can't log in — contact your system administrator to verify your account is approved.
  • My problem isn't showing up — make sure it was submitted successfully and check your department filter.
  • Location isn't being detected — ensure location permissions are enabled for the app in your device settings.
  • I'm not receiving notifications — check that notifications are enabled for the app in your device settings.

Contact Support

If you're experiencing an issue not covered above, please reach out and we'll get back to you as soon as possible.
