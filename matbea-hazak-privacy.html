Privacy Policy

מטבע הבזק – Budget Management App

Last updated: March 2026

Short version: מטבע הבזק is an internal organizational app. We do not collect, sell, or share your personal data with any third party. All data stays within your organization.

1. What This App Does

מטבע הבזק is a budget management application for internal organizational use. It allows authorized users to track unit budgets, submit and approve purchase requests, and communicate securely within the organization.

2. Data We Collect

  • Account credentials — username and a device-bound authentication token, used solely to verify your identity within the app.
  • Budget and request data — purchase requests and budget figures you enter, stored in a private Firebase Firestore database accessible only to authorized users.
  • Chat messages — messages between users are end-to-end encrypted before being stored. They cannot be read by anyone outside the conversation.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • No real name, email address, or phone number
  • No location data
  • No contacts or address book
  • No browsing history
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No health or financial data beyond what you explicitly enter in the app

4. Third-Party Services

The app uses Firebase (Google LLC) for authentication and data storage. Firebase may collect anonymous crash reports and basic usage diagnostics. No data is shared with any advertising or analytics platform. Firebase's privacy policy is available at firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

5. Data Security

All network communication is encrypted via TLS. Chat messages are end-to-end encrypted using AES-256-GCM with ephemeral ECDH P-256 key exchange, meaning only the intended recipient can decrypt them. Authentication uses a device-bound key stored in the device's secure enclave (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).

6. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as the organization requires it for operational purposes. User accounts can be removed by an administrator at any time.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for use by authorized adult personnel only. It is not directed at children under the age of 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes.

9. Contact

For any questions about this privacy policy, contact the app administrator within your organization.
